# Twelve Week Training Program 2

This document is the consolidated specification for the second 12-week training program developed from the late-program Garage review and subsequent planning discussion. It records the agreed training structure, exercise selection, progression rules, transition week, recovery strategy, equipment choices, core specialization, and safety constraints.

## DESIGN_RATIONALE

The previous Garage program produced a clear improvement in strict pull-up capacity, from an established 5 x 4 benchmark to a repeatable 6 x 4 benchmark. Weighted push-ups at 16 kg for 4 x 12 and bodyweight dips at 4 x 12 also became stable strength benchmarks.

The new program therefore does not replace the successful structure simply for variety. It increases specificity and progression where useful, gives core training a dedicated day, introduces weighted pulling progressively, and makes better use of the outdoor gym.

The user specifically prefers to train outdoors on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday for pull-up and core work, while Tuesday and Saturday remain home sessions. The program is therefore organized around that preference.

## MONDAY — PULL STRENGTH + CORE

Location: outdoor gym

Warm-up:
- 5–7 min general warm-up
- Arm circles
- Band pull-aparts
- Scapular pull-ups
- Easy dead hang

Main work:

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strict pull-ups | 5 x 4 initially | 90–120 sec |
| Weighted pull-ups | 3 x 3 when introduced | 2–3 min |
| Band rows | 3 x 12 | 60–90 sec |
| Scapular pull-ups | 3 x 10 | 60 sec |
| Garhammer hold | 3 x 10–15 sec | 60 sec |
| Front plank | 3 x 60 sec | 60 sec |
| Dead hang | 2 x 30 sec | 60 sec |

Important pull-up-volume refinement:

During the initial adaptation period, do not simply stack 5 x 4 bodyweight pull-ups plus 3 x 3 weighted pull-ups. Start with 5 x 4 bodyweight if weighted pull-ups are not yet qualified. Once weighted pull-ups are introduced, use approximately 3 x 4 strict bodyweight + 3 x 3 weighted rather than automatically increasing total pulling volume.

Weighted pull-ups start at approximately +2.5 kg only after bodyweight pull-up performance is repeatable with strict dead-hang starts, chin clearly over the bar, no leg swing, and approximately 1–2 reps in reserve. If form deteriorates, remain at the current level.

## TUESDAY — PUSH STRENGTH + LEGS

Location: home

Warm-up:
- Shoulder mobility
- Air squats x 20
- Easy push-ups x 10

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Weighted push-ups | 4 x 10–12 | 90–120 sec |
| Dips | 4 x 8–12 | 90–120 sec |
| Bulgarian split squats | 4 x 10 / leg | 90 sec |
| Pike push-ups | 3 x 8–10 | 90 sec |
| Calf raises | 3 x 20 | 60 sec |
| Side plank | 3 x 40 sec / side | 60 sec |

Progression:
- Weighted push-ups: maintain 16 kg until 4 x 12 is comfortably repeatable with clean form; then add approximately 1–2 kg.
- Dips: when bodyweight 4 x 12 is clean and repeatable, add approximately 2.5 kg and reset to 4 x 8.
- Pike push-ups: progress from 3 x 8 toward 3 x 12 before making the exercise harder.
- Bulgarian split squats: increase loading only after controlled 4 x 10 / leg is consistently solid.

## WEDNESDAY — CORE + GRIP + CONDITIONING

Location: outdoor gym

This is the dedicated core-specialization day.

### Core

| Exercise | Sets x Reps / Time |
|---|---:|
| Captain's-chair Garhammer raises | 3 x 8 |
| Captain's-chair knee raises | 3 x 10–12 |
| Hollow hold | 3 x 30–40 sec |
| Side plank | 3 x 40 sec / side |
| Dead bug | 3 x 12 / side |

The Saturday Captain's-chair leg-raise exercise is moved to Wednesday. It is not duplicated on Saturday.

Garhammer technique:
- Start with bent knees.
- Keep the movement controlled.
- Focus on posterior pelvic tilt / curling the pelvis rather than simply lifting the thighs.
- Avoid swinging.
- Do not take both raise variations to failure.
- If the Captain's-chair setup permits a clean Garhammer movement, this is preferred over forcing straight-leg raises.

### Grip

| Exercise | Sets x Reps / Time |
|---|---:|
| Dead hang | 3 x 30–40 sec |
| Active hang | 2 x 15–20 sec |

### Conditioning

- 15–20 min moderate aerobic work.
- Use a talk-test intensity: breathing elevated but conversation still possible.
- Walking, outdoor cardio, or similar moderate activity can be used.
- Do not turn Wednesday into a hard conditioning day that compromises Thursday pulling.

## THURSDAY — PULL HYPERTROPHY + CORE

Location: outdoor gym

| Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strict pull-ups | 4 x 5 | 90 sec |
| Band-assisted pull-ups | 2 x 8, only if recovery is good | 90 sec |
| Band rows | 4 x 12–15 | 60–90 sec |
| Negative pull-ups | optional 2 x 3, 5-sec lowering | 90 sec |
| Garhammer raises | 3 x 8 | 60 sec |
| Dead hang | 2 x 30 sec | 60 sec |

The assisted pull-ups are deliberately limited to 2 x 8. They are support work, not another large volume block.

Negative pull-ups are optional rather than compulsory. If pulling fatigue is high, omit them.

If the outdoor gym side pull-up bars provide a comfortable neutral grip, they can replace the assisted-pull-up work every other week rather than being added on top of it.

## PULL-UP_STANDARD

Every strict pull-up progression is judged by the same standard:
- dead-hang start
- chin clearly over the bar
- no leg swing
- controlled repetition
- no shortened range of motion

The previous established benchmark was 6 x 4 strict pull-ups, 24 total repetitions, with 60 sec rest. This is treated as a real training benchmark, not merely a one-session peak.

The new program aims to improve strength rather than simply accumulate more repetitions.

## PROGRESSION_RULES

General rule:
- Progress one variable at a time.
- Keep approximately 1–2 reps in reserve on normal work.
- Do not reduce rest periods merely to make an exercise harder.
- Do not add volume simply because a session feels easy once.
- Technique and repeatability must precede load increases.

Pull-ups:
- Start with 5 x 4 strict bodyweight.
- Work toward 5 x 5 only when quality is consistently excellent.
- When weighted pull-ups are qualified, use 3 x 4 bodyweight + 3 x 3 weighted rather than automatically adding all weighted work on top of 5 x 4.
- Weighted pull-ups begin around +2.5 kg.
- Progress weighted pull-ups toward 4 x 4–5 only when earned.

Weighted push-ups:
- Start at 16 kg, 4 x 10–12.
- Maintain 4 x 12 until clearly sustainable.
- Add approximately 1–2 kg.

Dips:
- Bodyweight 4 x 8–12.
- At a repeatable clean 4 x 12, add approximately 2.5 kg and reset to 4 x 8.

Pike push-ups:
- Progress 3 x 8 toward 3 x 12.
- Then increase difficulty rather than endlessly increasing repetitions.

Garhammer:
- Start with strict 3 x 8 raises and/or 10–15-sec holds where programmed.
- Progress toward 3 x 12 controlled repetitions or 30-sec holds.
- Quality pelvic control is more important than range obtained by swinging.

Captain's-chair knee raises:
- Progress from 3 x 10 toward 3 x 15.
- Keep movement controlled and stop before technique breaks down.

Hollow hold:
- Progress from 30 sec toward 45–60 sec.

Side plank:
- Progress from 40 sec toward 60 sec per side.

## TWELVE_WEEK_PHASES

### Weeks 1–3 — Adaptation
- Learn the new outdoor-gym setup.
- Introduce the Captain's-chair work carefully.
- Establish bodyweight pulling quality.
- Introduce weighted pull-ups only if the qualification criteria are met.
- Keep 1–2 reps in reserve.
- Do not chase maximums.

### Week 4 — Deload
- Reduce approximately 40–50% of normal set volume.
- Keep technique.
- No max testing.

### Weeks 5–7 — Progressive overload
- Gradually increase load, repetitions, or difficulty.
- Only one progression variable at a time.
- Continue strict pull-up standards.

### Week 8 — Deload
- Again reduce approximately 40–50% of normal set volume.
- Keep movement quality and recovery priority.

### Weeks 9–11 — Strength emphasis
- Prioritize weighted pull-ups where qualified.
- Maintain rather than unnecessarily increase accessory volume.
- Keep push and leg work productive but subordinate to pull-up quality.

### Week 12 — Assessment
Assess:
- strict pull-up performance
- weighted pull-up performance if established
- 16 kg weighted push-up benchmark and/or next sustainable load
- dip performance
- pike push-up performance
- Garhammer control/hold
- Captain's-chair raise performance
- hollow hold
- side plank

Do not turn every exercise into a maximal test. The main assessment remains strict pull-up strength and quality.

## FIVE_DAY_TRANSITION

The new program starts Monday 2026-10-12. Instead of a full seven-day transition, the transition was shortened to five days: Wednesday through Sunday.

The transition is not complete inactivity. Its purpose is to reduce fatigue, practice the new movement patterns lightly, and arrive at Monday recovered.

### Wednesday — Outdoor gym
Light core + grip:
- Captain's-chair Garhammer raises: 2 x 6–8, easy
- Hollow hold: 2 x 20 sec
- Dead hang: 2 x 20–30 sec
- Easy walk: 15–20 min

Skip conditioning if tired.

### Thursday — Outdoor gym
Light pull technique:
- Strict pull-ups: 3 x 3, comfortable
- Band rows: 2 x 12
- Scapular pull-ups: 2 x 8
- Dead hang: 2 x 20 sec

No weighted pull-ups, assisted-pull-up volume, or negatives.

### Friday
Rest. Normal daily activity or easy walking only.

### Saturday — Home
Light push + legs:
- Push-ups: 2 x 10 bodyweight
- Dips: 2 x 6–8 if shoulders feel comfortable
- Bodyweight squats: 2 x 12
- Reverse lunges: 2 x 8 / leg, bodyweight

No weighted backpack, tempo sets, or sets near failure.

### Sunday
Rest. Optional easy walk. Prepare equipment for Monday.

### Monday 2026-10-12
Begin the full 12-week program if recovered and comfortable.

## CORE_SPECIALIZATION

Core training is increased in quality and organization, not simply by adding unlimited volume.

The dedicated Wednesday session provides:
- trunk flexion / posterior pelvic tilt through Garhammer work
- controlled knee-raise work
- anti-extension through hollow holds and dead bugs
- lateral stability through side planks
- grip and hanging tolerance through dead and active hangs

Monday and Thursday contain shorter core elements so that the core is trained frequently without making every session a long abdominal workout.

The Saturday core work was reduced by moving the Captain's-chair exercise to Wednesday.

## OUTDOOR_GYM_EQUIPMENT

The outdoor gym provides:
- Captain's chair for Garhammer and knee raises
- side pull-up bars for additional grip/neutral-grip options
- pull-up bars for strict and weighted pull-ups

The side pull-up bars should only be used if the grip is comfortable and the shoulder position feels natural.

## EQUIPMENT_PRIORITIES

The most useful equipment additions are:
1. A secure adjustable weighted vest or other reliable loading system.
2. Fractional plates, approximately 0.5–1 kg increments, for gradual pull-up and dip progression.
3. Adjustable dumbbells are optional rather than essential.

The existing 16 kg backpack remains useful for weighted push-ups and squats.

## RECOVERY_AND_HEALTH

Health remains the first priority.

Monitor:
- sleep quality
- general energy
- joint comfort
- shoulder comfort
- lower-back comfort
- motivation
- warm-up quality
- grip recovery

If several recovery markers worsen for several days, reduce training volume by approximately 30–40% rather than forcing the planned workload.

The user previously experienced very faint lower-back discomfort during hanging leg raises. For new Garhammer and Captain's-chair work:
- begin with bent knees
- avoid swinging
- emphasize pelvic curl
- lower under control
- stop if pain appears rather than normal abdominal effort
- do not progress the movement while symptoms are present
- if discomfort persists or repeatedly returns, pause the provoking movement and consider assessment by a qualified physiotherapist.

For dips, avoid an unnecessarily deep range if the shoulders feel uncomfortable.

## AEROBIC_ACTIVITY

The program includes a moderate Wednesday conditioning component and allows walking and table tennis as additional activity.

The general health target remains approximately 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week when practical. Resistance-training sessions, walking, table tennis and moderate conditioning can all contribute, but extra hard conditioning is not required.

## TRAINING_PRINCIPLES

- Train properly before chasing repetition records.
- Pull-up quality is the primary progression metric.
- Health and recovery take priority over the schedule.
- The program should not be changed unnecessarily.
- Repeated successful performance is required before increasing difficulty.
- More exercises are not automatically better.
- More weekly days are useful only when recovery supports them.
- Wednesday is a core/grip/conditioning day, not a second hard pull day.
- Outdoor-gym days are used deliberately for pull-up and Captain's-chair work.
- Home days remain focused on pushing and legs.

## BASELINE_FOR_PROGRAM_START

Before or during Week 1, record:
- strict pull-ups: current clean set/repetition benchmark
- weighted pull-ups: baseline if introduced
- weighted push-ups: 16 kg benchmark
- dips: current clean benchmark
- pike push-ups: current clean benchmark
- Garhammer hold/raise capability
- Captain's-chair knee raises
- hollow hold
- side plank
- bodyweight and general recovery status

These baselines are for comparison at Week 12 and should not all be maximal tests.
